# 审稿意见（按问题类型归纳）

## 总体评价

This paper presents KAMT, a knowledge-augmented multimodal learning framework for diagnosis of Alzheimer's disease and related neurological disorders. The main idea is to construct a disease-oriented relational knowledge graph, DemRKG, by refining external biomedical knowledge and then use the resulting representations to guide multimodal fusion of EHR/clinical features, biomarkers, and MRI. The framework incorporates modality-specific knowledge retrieval, masked cross-attention, and an energy-constrained refinement mechanism. The use of several independent cohorts and the attempt to investigate different KG construction strategies are strengths. However, several aspects of the experimental design and methodology are insufficiently described, and some of the claims appear stronger than what the current experiments establish.

The topic is highly relevant to biomedical informatics, and the incorporation of domain knowledge into multimodal learning is intriguing. The experiments conducted across four datasets and the comparison of different knowledge graph configurations are notable strengths. However, I have several concerns:

Overall, the proposed knowledge-guided multimodal approach is interesting, and the evaluation across multiple datasets is a strength. However, clearer experimental methodology and stronger validation at the component level would enhance the reliability and reproducibility of the work.

## 任务定义与数据集适用范围

•       The definition of the prediction problem across the four datasets needs substantial clarification. ADNI, NACC, and AIBL involve NC/MCI/AD three-class classification, whereas PPMI involves Control/PD/SWEDD/Prodromal four-class classification. Thus, PPMI is not an ADRD diagnosis dataset and represents a substantially different neurological disease problem. The rationale for combining these tasks under the same ADRD framework should be explained more carefully.

## 数据划分与实验协议

•       There is a potentially important inconsistency in the data splitting strategy. Section IV-A states that all datasets are initially split 8:2 into training and test sets and that the training set is subsequently split 8:2 into training and validation sets. However, the NACC knowledge extraction section states that NACC is divided into training/test/validation sets using a 7:2:1 ratio.

1. Section IV-A first splits 80/20 and then splits the initial training partition 80/20, implying 64/16/20 train/validation/test. Section IV-B instead states 70/10/20 for NACC. The data-splitting protocol is not inconsistent.

1. Experimental protocol needs clarification: The manuscript initially describes an 8:2 split, followed by another 8:2 training/validation split. However, the NACC section later references a 7:2:1 split. This inconsistency should be addressed.

## 知识图谱与潜在数据泄漏

•       Potential information leakage through KG construction deserves much more rigorous examination. The manuscript states that clinical features are mapped to PrimeKG and that clinically agreed pathology nodes such as AD and APOE are explicitly introduced as seeds. At the patient level, abnormal clinical features are then used to activate phenotype nodes. Because some clinical assessments may be directly related to the diagnostic labels, the authors need to demonstrate that neither diagnosis-defining variables nor label-derived information enters the patient-specific KG representation. A detailed table showing all clinical variables used, their mapping to KG entities, and whether each variable contributes to clinical diagnosis would substantially improve confidence in the evaluation.

4. Potential data leakage problem remains unresolved. Some of the figures are not interpreted. For example fig. 2. What is found from the figure? What is the meaning of diferent color points. The quality of the paper needs more improvement.

## 性能提升与统计验证

•       The extraordinarily large improvement over the baselines requires stronger validation. For example, on ADNI, KAMT achieves an accuracy of 0.8299 and AUROC of 0.9456, compared with 0.5996/0.7535 for AlignMamba and 0.5676/0.6692 for CAMera. Similarly large gains occur for the other datasets. Such improvements are potentially important, but they also make it particularly important to establish that all methods use exactly the same subjects, modalities, preprocessing, train/test partitions, and hyperparameter-selection protocol. Statistical significance testing between KAMT and the strongest competing methods would also strengthen the conclusions.

2. Validation of performance improvements: The manuscript presents very large performance improvements that require stronger validation. For instance, KAMT reports an accuracy of 0.8299 for ADNI, compared to 0.5996 for AlignMamba and 0.5676 for CAMera. More detailed information about repeated runs, hyperparameter tuning, and statistical significance would enhance confidence in these results.

## 语言、术语与图表呈现

•       The manuscript would benefit from substantial language editing. There are numerous grammatical and terminology issues, including phrases such as “clinic metadata,” “in the diagnostic of ADRD,” “Clinic-guided KAMT,” and “clinic knowledge.” “Clinical” would generally be more appropriate. The terminology around “biomarkers” is also unclear: the BM modality appears to include clinical scales and biological states rather than a consistently defined biomarker modality.

5. Language and presentation: The manuscript would benefit from language editing and clearer presentation, as several sentences and claims are difficult to follow.
